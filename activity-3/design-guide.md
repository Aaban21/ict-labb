# TechTreasure Blog — Design Guide

## Theme

Cyberpunk neon: a dark digital backdrop with bright accents, used in a readable and welcoming way.

## Color palette

- Background: deep navy `#101426`
- Main text: soft white `#f4f6ff`
- Accent cyan: `#51e5ff`
- Accent magenta: `#ff55c8`
- Panel: blue charcoal `#1b223b`

## Typography

Use Arial or another clean sans-serif for body text. Use bold, high-contrast headings to give the pages a technology feel. Keep body text comfortably sized and easy to read.

## Layout strategy

Use a centered header, a narrow readable content column, and separate article cards. Keep navigation near the top on both pages. Use neon colors for links, labels, and small highlights rather than long passages of text.
